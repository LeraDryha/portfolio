# Surface brief: case study page (/work/<slug>), concept on Comfy

## Scope and mode
One template for every case study; first built and tested on Comfy, the richest case. Mode: Read (the visitor wants to understand what Lera did and how well).

## Audience, job, proof, constraints
- Recruiters and hiring managers who opened a case from the home page. They want the answer first: role, problem, what was done, result. Some keep reading for the process.
- Proof: real Notion content only. Comfy: 15 interviews, survey 178 of 385 target, 54 features across 6 competitors, usability tests (Maze 87.5% → 92.3% success; moderated completion 75% in the current flow, 67% in iteration 1, 100% in iteration 2), business goal 30% → 40% credit order share stated as a goal, not a result.
- Images are shown as they are in Notion, inside calm uniform frames with captions.
- Confirmed by the owner: summary first, then details; images as-is in calm frames; structure "Double Diamond as navigation".

## Direction contract
THESIS: The case is read through the process it actually followed: a Double Diamond index drawn as the page's navigation, after a summary sheet that answers the recruiter first. It refuses the category default of a cover image, a wall of screenshots and a "My role" eyebrow.
OWN-WORLD: DESIGN.md unchanged: paper ground, white tiles, Geist titles, Newsreader italic voice, Mulberry Ink only on evidence, the active phase and actions. The diamond is drawn in hairline Ink; the current half fills with Mulberry Ink.
STORY: In ten seconds the visitor knows Lera's role, the problem, what changed and the strongest number. Scrolling, the diamond shows where in the process they are; each phase reads as a calm column with framed figures; it ends on the outcome and the next case.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left index column: back link, then two vertical diamonds (Discover, Define / Develop, Deliver) with italic labels, Summary lit. Content: "Comfy" in Headline, one-line context, a facts row (role, team, year, business goal), three short blocks Problem / What we did / Result, and a full-width Proof strip "75% → 100%" (task completion, current flow vs redesign, moderated tests with 4 and 7 sessions). The first figure starts at the fold.
FORM: Double Diamond as navigation, position 7 of 7 on the ordered structure list, seed key 2064f068. Signature interaction: the active half of the diamond fills with Mulberry Ink in proportion to reading progress through that phase; on phones the same diamonds turn horizontal in a sticky top bar.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Recorded adaptations
- Facts row shows "Business goal" instead of "method": the drawn index already states the method, and the goal is labelled as a goal, not a result.
- The Proof mark is a full-width strip under the three blocks because a quarter-width tile could not hold it; it reads current flow vs redesign, the honest comparison.
- On phones the sticky bar drops phase labels (390px cannot fit them) and names only the current phase.
- The page closes on a second Proof strip (Maze 87.5% → 92.3%) before the next case.

## Unresolved
- Cases without a Double Diamond process (Prevently, GRX) need a phase set of their own; the index must accept any 3-5 phases.
- Case copy for the summary blocks is drafted from Notion and needs Lera's approval.
