### User needs (based on best practices) → Business goals

- **Start quickly, no commitment stress, avoid long forms** → ↑ activation rate, ↓ drop-off
- **Understand “Why am I here?” and “What’s in it for me?”** → ↑ conversion from install to active user
- **See immediate benefit, feel progress, reduce uncertainty** → ↑ activation & retention, ↓ churn during day 0–1
- **Feel understood, get tailored experience, not generic flow** → ↑ engagement, ↑ relevance of content, better data for segmentation
- **Save time, avoid overwhelm, get to value without friction** → ↑ completion rate, ↓ drop-off
- **Feel rewarded, motivated, see “wow effect”** → ↑ retention, ↑ emotional bond
- **Know where I am, reduce anxiety, sense of control** → ↑ onboarding completion
- **Feel safe sharing data, clear boundaries (“not medical advice”)** → ↑ credibility, ↑ user retention

Welcome Screen carousel presents the value proposition and a non-medical disclaimer.

### Base information

I separated the Base Info and Body Metrics steps so that on the Body Metrics step I can show the user their BMI right after they enter their parameters — creating that first wow effect. (Create early wins & celebrate them. Userpilot)

<Figure src="/work/onboarding-ab-testing/16.webp" width={1800} height={910} alt="Base information and body metrics with the BMI insight." caption="Base information and body metrics." />

### Health conditions

Personalisation+segmentation. For ex. - users with diagnosed conditions receive tailored articles, recommendations and alerts. With no history – get preventive tips and general health content. If risk patterns appear (e.g., blood pressure fluctuations + medical history) app can suggest seeking medical attention. As a wow effect too.

Screen asks “What would you like us to help with?” (track heart rate, get prevention tips, keep a diary for doctor) – purpose: collect goals to segment users and show them what value they can expect from the app.

<Figure src="/work/onboarding-ab-testing/17.webp" width={1800} height={910} alt="Health conditions and goals screens." caption="Health conditions and goals." />

First Measurement lets users quickly experience the product: measure, see insights, and feel a second wow-effect. (Guide users to their first “Aha! moment”. Userpilot)
