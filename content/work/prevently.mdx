## In short

- **Product discovery and logic.** Validated the idea with a survey of 350+ women and in-depth interviews, built a CJM and full SRS documentation. Designed a system of medical algorithms that adapts recommendations to age, family history, risk factors and existing diagnoses, edge cases included.
- **Build and launch.** Using my own algorithms and AI (Claude Code), built the core architecture, refined the UI/UX and released the MVP on the App Store on 14 July 2026.
- **Traction.** 2,500+ organic downloads with no marketing budget, a 5.0 rating from 37 App Store reviews, and an engaged audience on social media.
- **Iterations.** From early users’ feedback, designed and shipped a major update: wider personalisation with obstetric history and clinical screenings (tuberculosis, depression, anxiety), patient pathways, and action algorithms for red flags when handling sensitive medical information.
