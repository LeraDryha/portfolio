<Chapter id="learned" title="What I learned">

A medical product is mostly decisions about what not to say. The hardest rules to write were the ones that leave a check-up out, and each of them needed a source.

Going native twice costs every component twice. One rules engine and the same test profiles on both platforms kept the two apps telling the same truth.
