### UI competitor research

At this stage, I already know that the solution will be based on a UI familiar to similar websites, and the only thing left to define is the overall look and feel.

The client’s only requirement was to keep the green color, which distinguishes their center from others.

I created a mood board with screenshots of competitors, organized into the following categories:

1. Iconography
2. Typography
3. Color Scheme
4. Animation (Microinteractions)
5. Geometry
6. Tone of Voice

### Problem definition

1. Lack of a mobile-friendly version of the website
2. Unclear information about the regions served by the site
3. Difficulty finding information due to poor navigation and lack of guidance
4. Inconsistent design and layout across the site
5. Cluttered and overloaded website design

### Ideation

My favorite part of any design process is brainstorming. We worked as a team of designers, collaborating to tackle each problem creatively and effectively. Naturally, we followed all the brainstorming rules:

- No judgment
- No leading
- Embrace even the craziest ideas
- Focus on quantity over quality

### Design system

The design system was developed to ensure consistency, efficiency, and a cohesive user experience across the project.

By standardizing colors, typography, components, and interactions, the design system simplifies the design process and workflow, enabling faster iterations and easier maintenance as the project evolves.
