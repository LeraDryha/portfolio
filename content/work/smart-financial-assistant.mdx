<Chapter id="problem" n={1} of={5} title="I connected my accounts… and then what?">

Users were able to connect their bank accounts via Plaid, but the product failed to keep them engaged. After the initial setup, many users didn’t return or explore further features. This highlighted a clear gap: the experience lacked perceived value beyond basic transaction aggregation.

I needed to rethink how to turn raw financial data into something actionable, insightful, and worth revisiting.

<Chapter id="hypotheses" n={3} of={5} title="Hypotheses and validation">

<Goal>to turn our best guesses into experiments before we waste a sprint (or ten) 😂</Goal>

I formulated hypotheses for new recurring payments features based on user research and behavioral patterns and defined target Mixpanel events and success metrics for post-launch validation to help product and data teams measure real impact.

With the recurring payments module we were at a crossroads: we wanted everything at once, so the question was how to prioritise our resources and effort. In parallel I ran discovery: desk research, an analysis of Rocket Money, Monarch and Revolut, and then in-depth interviews, which gave clear insights: users don’t want to track their coffee, they want to know they aren’t being charged more than planned. I synthesised the data, mapped it onto user personas, prepared a CJM and ran a workshop with the team, where we defined the key opportunities and prioritised the focus on the recurring module. I wrote hypotheses so we could measure the impact of our decisions, built prototypes and ran usability testing.

### Edge cases: data accuracy

In personal finance products the biggest risks and constraints usually come from data accuracy. Wrong categorisation, missed or falsely detected recurring payments, transaction statuses (authorisation within 30 days, then capture), wrongly detected transfers, duplicate transactions or delayed data sync can badly distort analytics and budgets. We tried to work through each of these edge cases, so we could signal them to the user correctly and our system worked correctly. Plus, for more control and automation, we introduced a rules feature where users could manage their transactions.
