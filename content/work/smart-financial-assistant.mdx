## The problem

Users could connect their bank accounts via Plaid, but the product failed to keep them engaged. After the initial setup many didn’t return or explore further. The experience lacked perceived value beyond basic transaction aggregation.

<Figure src="/work/smart-financial-assistant/s03.jpg" width={1600} height={610} alt="User problems: no clear way to understand money flow, recurring payments, spending habits or context, and the question “I connected my accounts… and then what?”" caption="User problems, and the question behind them." />

## The challenge

The MVP offered a list of all transactions from connected accounts, filtering by account and category, category-based spending insights, and an overview of balances. It quickly proved insufficient for long-term engagement. As part of a small product team, I led the design as we began scaling the product.

## Process

Design Thinking, to keep the solution aligned with both user needs and business goals: desk research, in-depth interviews, competitive analysis, hypotheses, testing, and building on.

### What we learned from users

<Insight n={1} title="Viewing transactions alone isn’t valuable" quote="I already have banking apps, why would I use another just to look at the same list of transactions?">People trusted their bank apps more and didn’t want to duplicate them.</Insight>
<Insight n={2} title="Users want control over recurring payments" quote="I want to track my subscriptions, mortgage, and insurance, not every cup of coffee.">They cared about regular, essential commitments more than everyday spending.</Insight>
<Insight n={3} title="Fear of price changes or unexpected charges" quote="What if Netflix suddenly charges more and I don’t notice?">They wanted cues about unusual patterns in recurring charges.</Insight>
<Insight n={4} title="Need to anticipate what’s coming next" quote="Show me which bills are coming soon and from which card.">Predictability was key: upcoming payments by date and source.</Insight>
<Insight n={5} title="Desire for spending breakdowns" quote="I’d love to see how much I spend on subscriptions vs. insurance.">Category-level analytics on recurring expenses, in % and in money.</Insight>
<Insight n={6} title="Poor feedback after account connection" quote="I connected my account... and nothing happened. Did it even work?">No confirmation or next step after Plaid led to drop-off.</Insight>

## Hypotheses and validation

I formulated hypotheses for new recurring-payment features from research and behaviour, and defined the Mixpanel events and success metrics so product and data teams could measure the real impact after launch.
