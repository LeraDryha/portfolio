## Reports information architecture

Reports were rebuilt around three places: an Overview for the daily check, Statistic API Access, and My Reports for saved, template and scheduled reports, each with a clear way to create a new one.

<Figure src="/work/adtelligent/r02.jpg" width={1600} height={755} alt="Map of the updated reports structure: Overview with charts, table and create report; Statistic API Access; My Reports with favourites, templates, current and scheduled reports, and create report." caption="Updated reports information architecture." />

## Overview

### What users need

Users log in every day to check the volume of Ad Requests, Ad Opportunities and Impressions, and ad spend (Spent and eCPM), broken down by campaigns, sites and countries.

### Ideas

- A dedicated Overview page for quick data visualisation: charts are easier and faster to read than tables.
- Flexible chart filters: by campaigns, sites and countries, plus dates.
- The usual data table on the overview, with quick view settings and export.
- Sorting icons on table pills, so users see the available actions right away.

## My Reports

### What users need

- A convenient way to save reports and add them to favourites for quick access to important data.
- Ready-made report sets (templates) to create new reports from proven setups.
- Navigation between report types: current reports, including saved ones, and scheduled reports sent by email.

### Ideas

- A dedicated reports page with grouping, search and filters.
- A Create report button that starts from a saved report or a template.
- A list and grid switch, for easier scanning of the reports.

## Creating a report

### What users need

When creating a report, users want to visualise the table after applying filters, manage and customise tables and charts, and save and export the report.

### Ideas

- Group metrics by meaning into separate charts instead of one mixed table: volumes (Ad Requests, Ad Opportunities, Impressions), money (Spent, eCPM) and percentages (Fill Rate by Ad Requests and by Ad Opportunities).
- Let people name a report as they create it, so the list isn’t full of “New Report”.

<Figure src="/work/adtelligent/r09.jpg" width={1600} height={1075} alt="Report builder: a table with a settings panel on the right." caption="Report builder, first version." />

- A side panel brings every table setting into one place: columns, metrics and filter pills, so people see and change all parameters at once.
- A plus next to each section title follows the pattern of most systems with complex settings. Selected filters move to the top of the list, so applied parameters are easy to review.
