<Chapter id="next" n={5} of={5} label="Next steps" title="What I’d do next">

- **A UI kit for the product**, to keep every part of the system consistent. The screens use elements and styles taken from the existing product.
- **Better charts.** Some of them may look illogical: I didn’t fully understand how some metrics work or which pains and gains they cover. With a detailed analysis of the metrics and user needs, the charts can be more useful and easier to read.
- **Responsive layouts.** Dashboards and tables like these often break on small screens, so every size needs working through, whatever device people use.

Some parts of the logic may be missing for lack of details and insights, which is why each decision above comes with my reasoning.

</Chapter>
