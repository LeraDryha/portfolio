<Chapter id="discover" n={1} of={6} label="Research" title="Discover" sub="Desk research, a kick-off with the business, competitors, 15 interviews and a survey.">

COMFY is the largest retailer of home appliances and electronics in Ukraine. It has run an omnichannel business for over 10 years and is the #2 player in Ukrainian e-commerce.

### Desk research

Before the kick-off meeting, we explored the context from several angles to prepare informed questions and spot early opportunities:

- Analyzed traffic data and user behavior using SimilarWeb.
- Reviewed customer reviews to find recurring complaints and concerns.
- Studied Comfy’s credit purchase flow against competitors to spot UX patterns.

<Figure src="/work/comfy/c03.jpg" width={1600} height={1133} alt="Desk research board: traffic data, customer reviews and the existing credit flow." caption="Desk research board." />

### What the business told us

- Not every product or service is eligible for credit; it depends on vendor agreements.
- Adding services to a purchase, such as setup or warranty, can unlock better terms: 12 months instead of 7.
- The company works with several banks to offer installments.
- People choose credit for different reasons: some lack the funds, others have the money but prefer to spread the cost.

### What needs to be better

- Credit terms and payment conditions were often unclear or overly complex.
- Users struggled to understand how the final amount was calculated.
- There was no guidance on choosing the most favourable bank offer.

### Follow-up survey

To check interview insights at scale, we turned recurring themes into survey questions. For a 95% confidence level we needed 385 respondents; the survey closed at 179, so its numbers are a direction, not proof.

The survey tested four insights: clarity of information, package deals, comparing credit terms, and wanting to see a product in person before buying.
