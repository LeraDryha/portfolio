This design project was developed to support the many Ukrainians experiencing mental health challenges, especially since the war began. It offers valuable information about their conditions and provides practical tools to help them find relief.

Design of a service to support mental health. The project focused on improving the user experience for individuals seeking emotional support and self-development resources. The process included user research, persona creation, customer journey mapping, and prototyping to ensure intuitive navigation and access to valuable resources. The Atomic Design approach was used to develop the UI kit, ensuring consistency and scalability.

### 1. Difficulty understanding and managing emotions, leading to a sense of losing control.

Coping cards help users reframe negative thoughts by pairing automatic, distressing thoughts with rational responses. This tool, inspired by cognitive-behavioral therapy, supports emotional regulation, helping users feel more in control during moments of anxiety.

### 2. Difficulty accessing clear, reliable mental health information from trusted sources. Lack of tools to track and monitor mental health progress.

The app offers clinically validated tests for users to assess mental health, understand symptoms, and track progress over time. Visual progress tracking helps users observe changes and make informed decisions about their well-being.

### 3. Lack of support and guidance during panic attacks. Lack of empathy and understanding from others.

The app offers a "Hotlines" feature, providing users with immediate access to trusted helplines and specialists for real-time support. Users can connect with verified mental health professionals who provide empathy, guidance, and expert assistance.

This feature addresses the need for timely support, helping users feel understood and supported during moments of distress.

### 4. Lack of empathy and understanding from others.

The "Community" feature fosters a supportive environment where users can share experiences, seek inspiration, and find comfort in stories from others facing similar challenges.

The "Ask an Expert" option, available with a premium subscription, allows users to receive guidance from mental health professionals, providing trusted insights and empathy to address their concerns.

### 5. Lack of tools to track and monitor mental health progress.

The app’s Diary and Mood Tracking features enable users to log daily emotions, track mood patterns, and reflect on their mental health journey. By recording feelings, surrounding factors, and gratitude entries, users gain insights into their mental health progress and identify trends, supporting better self-awareness and emotional management.

### Gamification

I incorporate gamification into the app to encourage consistent engagement, making mental health management feel approachable and rewarding. By offering achievements, progress tracking, and rewards for completing tasks, users are motivated to use the app regularly, reinforcing healthy habits.

Gamification helps transform mental health practices into a more interactive and enjoyable experience, fostering long-term commitment to self-care and emotional well-being.

<Chapter id="strategy" n={5} of={5} title="Strategy">

### Initial scale

A strong start would be to collaborate with mental health professionals—psychologists, psychotherapists, and neurologists—to recommend the app as a tool for journaling, tracking health dynamics and triggers, and using coping cards to reinforce rational thoughts

### Monetisation

Core features that address user pain points should be free to gather feedback and assess their value. Future monetization could include paid features such as:

- Specialist interaction within the community through a "question-answer" format.
- Access to specialist contacts for booking appointments, with potential discounts through the app.
- Access to video lectures and online masterclasses on mental health topics.
